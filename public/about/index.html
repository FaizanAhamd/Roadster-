About Roadster Loop

Roadster Loop — Night Run is a browser-based 3D driving and stunt game built around a night-time ramp course. The player accelerates a sports car toward a launch ramp, reacts to the launch cue, controls the car in the air, and aims to complete a clean landing while collecting rings and earning a score.

What the game is about

Each run combines timing, speed management, steering, aerial control, and landing technique. A run can end in a clean landing or a wipeout. The results screen shows a score and performance metrics so players can review the attempt and try to improve their personal best.

Designed for replay

Players can restart a run, adjust supported visual settings, choose a car paint colour, change camera views, and enable or mute sound. Personal game preferences and best-score information are stored locally in the browser.
