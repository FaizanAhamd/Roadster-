Game Features

Roadster Loop — Night Run is a compact 3D stunt-driving experience focused on repeatable runs and measurable performance.

3D night-time stunt course

Drive toward a ramp in a night-time environment, launch into the air, and attempt to complete the stunt.

Timing and launch reaction

A countdown and launch cue make reaction timing part of each attempt. Starting too early can affect the run.

Aerial control and stunts

Steer the car during flight, perform flips, and collect rings along the course.

Run scoring and results

Review score, personal best, reaction time, ramp speed, peak G-force, maximum altitude, airtime, flips, rings, and landing outcome.

Player options

  • Selectable car paint colours
  • Graphics quality setting
  • Multiple camera views
  • Sound on/off
  • Pause, restart, and score sharing/export actions
