Frequently Asked Questions

Is Roadster Loop free to play?

The game is available to play in a modern web browser at no charge.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation is required to play in a supported browser. The site may also offer browser-based installation where the browser supports it.

How is my best score saved?

The game stores the personal best and selected preferences in your browser's local storage. Clearing browser site data may remove these values.

Why did my run end in a wipeout?

A run may end in a wipeout when the car does not complete a stable landing. Review the result metrics and adjust your launch timing and aerial control on the next attempt.

Can I change the camera or graphics?

Use the in-game options/settings to select an available camera view and graphics quality.

Does the website use advertising?

Roadster Loop includes Google AdSense advertising technology. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and, where enabled, ad personalisation. See the Privacy Policy for details and controls.

How can I report a problem?

Use the Contact page to open the project's public issue tracker.
