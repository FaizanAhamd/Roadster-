How to Play

Roadster Loop is a timing-based stunt-driving game. Your goal is to build speed, launch at the right moment, control the car during flight, and land cleanly.

1. Start a run

Start a run from the game's title screen. Follow the countdown and wait for the launch cue. Reacting too early can affect the run.

2. Control the car

  • Steering: use the on-screen left and right controls, or the keyboard controls shown in the game.
  • Launch and thrust: use the launch/thruster control when the game prompts you.
  • Pause: use the in-game menu or the P/Escape shortcut when supported.
  • Camera: choose an available camera view from the options menu.

3. Complete the stunt

During flight, manage the car's attitude and use the available controls to perform flips and pass through rings. Aim for a stable touchdown rather than simply maximizing airtime.

4. Review your result

The results screen reports your score and run metrics, including reaction time, ramp speed, peak G, altitude, airtime, flips, and rings. Restart to try another run or use the available share/export actions.

Tip: Learn the timing first, then focus on aerial control and a clean landing.
