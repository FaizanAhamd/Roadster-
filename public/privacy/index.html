Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Roadster Loop — Night Run handles information when you visit and play the browser game at roadster-iota.vercel.app.

Game preferences stored in your browser

The game uses your browser's local storage to remember game-related settings such as your personal best score, selected car colour, sound preference, graphics quality, and tutorial status. This information is stored on your device and can be removed by clearing the site's data in your browser settings.

Advertising is provided by Google and may involve Google's advertising partners. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy policies and settings. This website does not control third-party cookies or identifiers.

This game is not designed to request personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern about information handled through advertising technologies, review Google's family and advertising privacy resources and contact us through the support link.
