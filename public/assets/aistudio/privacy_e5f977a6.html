PLAYER INFORMATION

Privacy policy

Roadster Loop is a browser-based fan game. This version does not require an account and does not send gameplay data to a game server.

Local storage

Your best score may be stored in your browser using localStorage so it can be shown on later visits. You can clear it by clearing this site's browser data. If browser storage is unavailable, the game continues without saving a score.

Third-party resources

The game loads the Three.js library from cdnjs. Your browser may connect to that CDN to retrieve the library; its use is subject to the CDN provider's policies. No analytics or advertising scripts are included in this starter project.

Contact and changes

This is an independent demo with no account or contact form. This policy may be updated if the game adds services that process data.
